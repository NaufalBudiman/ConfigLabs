Rack Builder

Plan racks to scale

Drag H3C, Cisco and generic equipment into a true-scale 19-inch rack, then place the racks in a room with hot and cold aisles. Power, weight, clearance and cooling are checked as you go, and problems are listed so you can fix them before install day.

  • Drag-and-drop rack elevation, front and rear
  • Power A/B, weight and floor loading checks
  • Issue list: overlaps, gaps, duplicate names and IPs
  • Room plan and 3D view with aisle and clearance checks
  • Export PNG, PDF, SVG, BOM CSV or a project file

What's new

Regular releases. Latest first; tap a release for details.

v7.10.0

Config Translator on your Dashboard

Every translation is now kept in this browser and can be reopened with its input, output and report. Save to My Files turns a translated config into an H3C config in your account that opens in the Builder, so you can keep editing it there. The Dashboard has a Config Translator card with today's free translations and your last translation, a Translations tile, and a Translations filter in Recent work.

v7.9.0

Liquid glass everywhere, one menu on every page

Every page now has the liquid-glass look of the Dashboard: glass panels, cards, toolbars and dialogs over soft colour glows, in dark and light. The top menu is now exactly the same on every page, with Log in or your account menu and the theme switch in one place, and your theme choice follows you from page to page. Code output, inputs and the drawing areas of the WiFi Planner, Topology and Rack Builder stay solid so they are easy to read.

v7.8.0

Dashboard: all your work in one place

A new Dashboard page brings your saved configs, WiFi projects, topology diagrams and rack projects together, with a Continue where you left off card, today's Translator usage and quick actions. Liquid-glass design in dark and light, built for phones too. Diagrams and racks saved in this browser show up even when you are not logged in. The Builder can now open a saved config from a link, WiFi projects that live only in your account open from a link too, and logging in from the Dashboard brings you back to it.

v7.7.0

Config Translator: Cisco and Ruijie to H3C

Paste a Cisco IOS / IOS-XE or Ruijie RGOS config and get an H3C Comware config. VLANs, trunks, LAG, SVIs, DHCP, static routes, OSPF, ACLs (renumbered for Comware), VRRP/HSRP, SNMP, SSH and NAT overload are translated. A report lists every line that was not translated or needs a second look, plus the interface and ACL renaming. Free: 3 translations a day, up to 150 lines. The H3C Config Builder now also supports LAG, VRRP, ACL ports and names, SNMP, SSH and OSPF multi-area.

v7.6.0

Config Builder: Ruijie

Ruijie (RGOS) is now a vendor in the Config Builder, with three platforms: switches (VLAN, trunk, LAG, VRRP, OSPF, ACL), routers (dot1Q sub-interfaces, NAT, DHCP, routing) and WLAN AC with fit APs (SSIDs, AP groups, WPA2-PSK, DHCP option 138). Nine templates included. Syntax follows public Ruijie documentation, so test on your model first.

v7.5.0

Rack Builder: polish

Press ? to see every shortcut (C cable tool, F fit, V front/rear, G gaps). The Core and Compute templates now come with cabling and power cords. Cable schedules export to PDF, PNG, SVG and CSV, and large projects (600+ devices) stay fast.

v7.4.0

Rack Builder: power cords, cooling and hand-offs

Draw power cords from device PSUs to PDU outlets: devices then take their A or B feed from the PDU, and each PDU is checked against its capacity. Auto power cords wires a whole rack in one click. Racks now show kW, BTU/h and the airflow needed, with a warning above about 10 kW. Send the cabled devices to the Topology editor as a ready diagram, or open a switch in the Config Builder with its hostname and interface descriptions filled in from its cables.

v7.3.0

Rack Builder: cabling

Connect any port to any port with the Cable tool (press C): pick the cable type (Cat5e/6/6A, DAC, AOC, OM3/OM4/OS2 fiber, console, USB), colour, length and label. Cables are drawn on the rack front and rear, with warnings for a wrong cable type, a port used twice and a cable that is too long. Includes a cable schedule (CSV, PDF) and a purchase list by type and length.

v7.2.1

Rack Builder: vendor-accurate device looks

H3C switches and servers are now drawn in black and Cisco in grey, like the real hardware. The category colour moved to a thin line on top (toggle: Tag). H3C S6860 now has its console, management ports, four fan trays and two PSUs on the rear. H3C servers get the UniServer ear layout (VGA, Type-C, USB on the left; power, UID, health on the right), a Bezel toggle and serial labels.

v7.2.0

Rack Builder: realistic device faceplates

Switches, firewalls, servers, storage, patch panels, PDUs and UPS units are now drawn like the real hardware, front and rear: port blocks with numbering, console and LEDs, drive bays and carriers, power supply and fan modules, PCIe slots. Each device shows a front and rear preview in its panel, and the equipment list shows a thumbnail. Port counts and types follow each model; LED and module positions are illustrative. Every port now has an ID, ready for cabling in the next version.

v7.1.5

Config Builder: DHCP CIDR dropdown

The CIDR field in each DHCP pool is now a dropdown (/8 to /32) that lists how many usable hosts each size gives, for example "/24 - 254 hosts", and shows the pool size under the form. If you list excluded IPs, it also shows how many users are left. This is display only; the generated config is unchanged.

v7.1.3

Rack Builder: snap tooltip

The Snap menu in Room mode now explains the shortcuts: hold Alt while dragging for free placement, or Shift to snap to floor tiles.

v7.1.2

Rack Builder: rename hint

Rack tabs now show a "Double-click to rename" tooltip, so naming racks is easier to discover.

v7.1.1

Rack Builder: floor tiles, rack types, names

Rooms now have a tile grid (600 mm squares with row and column labels) and racks and objects snap into the tiles and stay inside the walls. Pick a rack type from a catalogue (cabinet, open frame, wall mount) and any height in U, name each rack, and see the server count per rack and for the whole project. Fixes: duplicated racks no longer land on top of the original, double-click rename works, the first click after editing a field is no longer lost, and the editor now fits phone screens.

v7.1.0

Rack Builder: Room plan and 3D

Rack Builder now has a Room mode. Set the room size, place racks and objects (doors, cooling units, columns) on a to-scale plan, and drag them with snapping. Racks arrange into hot and cold aisles with one click, and the plan checks clearance in front and behind each rack, door swing, racks facing the wrong way, and the room heat load against your cooling capacity. A 3D view lets you orbit the room. Export the plan or the 3D view as PNG, PDF or SVG.

v7.0.0

Rack Builder (beta)

A new tool for planning server racks to scale: drag H3C, Cisco and generic equipment into a 19-inch rack, see the front and rear, and check power per feed, loss of a feed, rack weight and floor loading. Open gaps, overlaps and duplicate names or IPs are listed as issues. Export PNG, PDF, SVG, a bill of materials (CSV) or a project file. Values marked Typical are placeholders: check them against the datasheet.

v6.3.5

H3C config accuracy

H3C output now follows the official Comware 7 command reference: DHCP forbidden-ip moved to system view, banner delimiters, common DHCP pools no longer emit an extended-pool apply command, and VLAN templates fill both Name and Description.

v6.3.3

Topology: Export Everywhere

JPEG, WebP and PDF downloads are now also in the WiFi Planner view, and every diagram on the My diagrams page has an Export image / PDF option in its menu that opens the export dialog directly.

v6.3.2

Topology (beta): Export

The Topology editor is now labelled Beta. Export has its own dialog with a live preview: PNG, JPEG, WebP, SVG, PDF (A4, A3, Letter or fit-to-diagram), draw.io for editing in diagrams.net, and a .json backup. Choose resolution, theme, transparent background and whether to include the title and legend.
